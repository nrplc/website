Bylaws
of
New River Public Library Cooperative
Governing Board
Revised March 2006
Article I: Name

This organization shall be called "The New River Public Library Cooperative."

Article II: Purpose

The purpose of the New River Public Library Cooperative is to provide unified and coordinated library service; to provide for equal access to public library service, to formulate and implement consistent and uniform plans, programs, policies, and procedures in the operation, maintenance, and development of library service throughout the legal service areas of the member counties.

Article III: Membership

The New River Public Library Cooperative is comprised of the counties of Baker, Bradford, and Union.


The Governing Board shall consist of six County Commissioners. Two Commissioners from each of the member counties shall comprise the Governing Board. Commissioners shall be appointed by each County's Chairman at their annual meeting.


Members shall attend all regularly scheduled meetings. Members who miss two consecutive meetings shall be notified by the Chairperson or Vice-Chairperson that he/she is not meeting attendance requirements. The Chairperson or Vice-Chairperson may then notify the Board of County Commissioners of that County to recommend the replacement of said member after he/she has been duly notified in writing by the Secretary that such action is pending. Any vacancy occurring during the unexpired term of office of any member shall be filled by the County Commissioners of that County, by appointment of a new member for the remainder of the vacant term. Such vacancy shall be filled within thirty (30) days of its occurrence.

Article IV: Officers
Section 1. The officers shall be a Chairperson, a Vice-Chairperson, and Secretary/Treasurer. There will be one officer from each county represented.
Section 2. The officers shall be elected for a term of one year at the annual meeting. Vacancies shall be filled by ballot at the next regular meeting of the Board after the vacancy occurs.
Section 3. The Chairperson shall preside at all meetings of the Board, authorize calls for any special meetings, appoint all committees, execute all documents authorized by the Board, serve as an ex-officio member of all committees and all duties associated with the office.
Section 4. The Vice-Chairperson shall assume and perform the duties and functions of the Chairperson in the event of the absence or disability of the Chairperson or of a vacancy in that office.
Article V: Meetings
Section 1. The regular meetings shall be held each quarter, the date and hour to be set by the Board at its annual meeting. The December meeting shall be the annual meeting.
Section 2. Special meetings may be called by the Chairperson or upon request of three members for the transaction of business as stated in the call. Notice stating the time and place of any special meeting and the purpose shall be given each member of the Board.
Section 3. Emergency action between meetings shall be decided by the single administrative head of the cooperative, Chairperson, and two (2) available members.
Section 4. The Board shall consider a quorum present when four (4) or more members are in attendance, one of which is the Chairperson or is the Vice-Chairperson acting in the absence of the Chairperson.
Section 5. The order of business at the regular meeting shall be as follows:
  1. Call to order.
  2. Approval of minutes for regular meeting and any special meetings (either read or previously received).
  3. Comments from the public.
  4. Administrative head's financial report of the cooperative.
  5. Unfinished business.
  6. New business.
  7. Comments from directors.
  8. Comments from Commissioners.
  9. Adjournment.
Section 6. Proceedings of all meetings shall be governed by Robert's Rules of Order, latest edition.
Article VI: Single Administrative Head and Staff
Section 1. The single administrative head shall have charge of the administration of the cooperative under the direction and review of the Governing Board. The single administrative head shall be held responsible for the care and maintenance of all cooperative property, for the efficiency of the cooperative's service to the public and its member libraries, and for the operation of the cooperative under the financial conditions set forth in the annual budget. The single administrative head shall attend all meetings of the Governing Board except those at which his/her appointment, salary, or performance is to be discussed or decided.
Section 2. The single administrative head shall recommend to the Governing Board the appointment and duties of the other employees and shall be held responsible for the proper direction and supervision of the staff.
Article VII: Committees
Section 1. The Chairperson shall appoint committees of one or more members each for such specific purposes as the business of the Board may require from time to time. The committee shall be considered to be discharged upon the completion of the purpose for which it was appointed and after the final report is made to the Board.
Section 2. All committees shall make a progress report to the Governing Board at each of its meetings.
Section 3. No committee will have other than advisory powers unless by suitable action of the Governing Board, it is granted specific power to act.
Article VIII: General
Section 1. An affirmative vote of the majority of all members of the Governing Board present at the time shall be necessary to approve any action before the Governing Board. The Chairperson may vote upon and may move or second a proposal before the Board.
Section 2. The Bylaws may be amended by the majority vote of all members of the Governing Board provided written notice of the proposed amendment shall have been mailed to all members at least ten (10) days prior to the meeting at which such action is proposed to be taken.
Section 3. Any rule or resolution of the Governing Board, whether contained in these Bylaws or otherwise, may be suspended temporarily in connection with business at hand, but such suspension, to be valid, may be taken only at a meeting at which two-thirds (6) of the members of the board shall be present and two-thirds of those present shall so approve.
The above Bylaws adopted by the Governing Board,
New River Public Library Cooperative.
