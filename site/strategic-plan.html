Strategic Plan

A five-year framework for library service across Baker, Bradford, and Union Counties.

History

New River Public Library Cooperative was established in 1996 to further resource sharing and foster cooperation between the libraries in Baker, Bradford and Union Counties in Florida. For nearly three decades NRPLC has provided shared automation, technology, outreach, and administrative services that allow each member library to serve its community more effectively than it could alone.

Vision

The libraries will be integral and indispensable to our communities by fostering creativity, discovery, openness, collaboration, and innovation.

Mission

New River Public Library Cooperative and its member libraries in Baker, Bradford, and Union Counties will be a gateway to lifelong learning and discovery. The libraries will provide quality materials, information, and resources in a comfortable environment that encourages community and individual growth.

Library Values

Equal access to information and resources

Respect for diversity

Commitment to lifelong learning

Safe, fun space to meet and gather with friendly, helpful staff

Service Objectives

Stimulate Imagination

The libraries will provide materials for the reading, viewing, and listening pleasure of the communities.

Satisfy Curiosity

Residents will have the resources they need to explore topics of personal interest and lifelong learning.

Encourage Early Literacy

Parents and children will have access to fun and engaging programs, services, and materials.

Connect to the Online World

The libraries will provide high-speed internet access and technology assistance, as well as online reading materials and databases for research.

Foster Community Collaboration

The libraries will be a central hub of activity, joining forces with community organizations to provide enriching, entertaining, and educational programs.

Governance

The New River Public Library Cooperative is an independent special district governed by a Board of Directors consisting of two county commissioners from each member county.
